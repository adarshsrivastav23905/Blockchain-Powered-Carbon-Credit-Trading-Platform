# Project Report: Blockchain-Powered Carbon Credit Trading Platform

---

## Abstract

This report presents a blockchain-based carbon credit trading platform built using Solidity smart contracts, Hardhat development framework, and React frontend. The platform simulates the complete lifecycle of carbon credits — from issuance by authorized entities to marketplace trading, ownership transfer, and irreversible retirement. By leveraging Ethereum's immutable ledger, the system addresses critical challenges in traditional carbon markets including double counting, opaque ownership, and unverifiable retirement claims. The project demonstrates key blockchain concepts including tokenization, role-based access control, atomic transactions, event-driven audit trails, and state machine design. All carbon credits in this project are simulated for educational purposes and do not represent legally verified emission reductions.

---

## 1. Introduction

Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges of the 21st century. Carbon credit markets have emerged as a market-based mechanism to incentivize emission reductions by putting a price on carbon. However, traditional carbon markets suffer from transparency issues, double counting, and settlement inefficiencies. Blockchain technology, with its inherent properties of immutability, transparency, and decentralization, offers a compelling solution to these challenges.

This project demonstrates how blockchain can be applied to carbon credit trading by building a functional prototype that simulates the entire credit lifecycle.

---

## 2. Carbon Credits

A carbon credit represents one metric tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO₂e) that has been reduced, avoided, or removed from the atmosphere. Credits are generated by projects such as:
- Renewable energy installations (solar, wind, hydro)
- Reforestation and afforestation
- Methane capture from landfills
- Energy efficiency improvements

Credits are traded in two types of markets:
- **Compliance Markets**: Government-regulated (e.g., EU Emissions Trading System)
- **Voluntary Markets**: Corporate voluntary offsetting (e.g., Verra, Gold Standard)

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Traditional carbon credit markets face several challenges:

1. **Double Counting**: The same credit is sold to multiple buyers
2. **Lack of Transparency**: Opaque ownership and transfer history
3. **Fraud**: Fake credits from non-existent projects
4. **Slow Settlement**: Manual verification and bank transfers
5. **Greenwashing**: Unverifiable retirement claims
6. **Poor Traceability**: Difficult to trace credit from creation to retirement

## 5. Challenges in Current Systems

- Centralized registries are single points of failure
- Cross-registry tracking is difficult
- Settlement takes days or weeks
- Verification is manual and expensive
- Retirement records can theoretically be altered by registry administrators

---

## 6. Proposed Blockchain Solution

Our platform uses Ethereum smart contracts to create a transparent, tamper-proof carbon credit trading system:

- **Unique Credit IDs**: Each credit has a unique on-chain identifier
- **Single Ownership**: Only one address owns a credit at any time
- **Atomic Trading**: Payment and ownership transfer happen simultaneously
- **Immutable Retirement**: Once retired, credits can never be re-traded
- **Event-Based Audit Trail**: Every action is permanently logged
- **Role-Based Access**: Only authorized issuers can create credits

## 10. Credit Data Model

The `CarbonCredit` struct contains: creditId, projectName, projectType, country, vintageYear, tonnesCO2e, issuer, owner, metadataHash, status, createdAt, retiredAt, retirementReason.

Status values: ACTIVE (0), LISTED (1), RETIRED (2).

---

## 11. Issuance Logic

- Only authorized issuers can create credits
- Tonnes CO₂e must be greater than zero
- Owner address must be valid (non-zero)
- Status starts as ACTIVE
- `CreditIssued` event is emitted

---

## 12. Trading Logic

- Only owners can list credits for sale
- Retired credits cannot be listed
- Price must be greater than zero
- Buyer must send exact price amount
- Ownership transfers atomically with payment
- Listings close after purchase

---

## 13. Retirement Logic

- Only current owner can retire
- Retirement is irreversible
- Timestamp and reason are recorded
- Retired credits cannot be transferred or sold
- `CreditRetired` event is emitted

---

## 14. Smart Contract Design

The contract implements:
- **3 modifiers**: onlyAdmin, onlyIssuer, onlyCreditOwner
- **8 functions**: registerIssuer, issueCarbonCredit, getCreditDetails, listCreditForSale, cancelListing, buyCredit, transferCredit, retireCredit
- **7 events**: IssuerRegistered, CreditIssued, CreditListed, ListingCancelled, CreditPurchased, CreditTransferred, CreditRetired

---

## 15. Security

- **Role-Based Access Control**: Modifiers enforce permissions
- **Checks-Effects-Interactions**: State updated before external calls
- **Reentrancy Guard**: Boolean lock on buyCredit
- **Input Validation**: require() checks on all inputs
- **State Machine**: Enum enforces valid lifecycle transitions

---

## 16. Market Integrity

- Unique credit IDs prevent duplication
- Single ownership prevents double counting
- Immutable retirement prevents re-use
- Event trail enables complete audit
- Authorization prevents fake issuance

## 23. Limitations

- **Cannot verify real-world emissions**: Blockchain tracks ownership but can't prove actual CO₂ reduction
- **Simulated data**: All credits are simulated — not connected to real registries
- **Simple marketplace**: Fixed-price listings only, no order books
- **Single contract**: Not scalable for production use
- **No oracle integration**: Cannot bring real-world monitoring data on-chain
- **Gas costs**: On-chain operations have costs on mainnet

## 25. Conclusion

This project successfully demonstrates how blockchain technology can address critical challenges in carbon credit markets. By implementing tokenization, role-based access control, atomic marketplace transactions, and irreversible retirement, the platform provides a transparent and tamper-proof system for carbon credit management.
